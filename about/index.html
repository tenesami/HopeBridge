About HopeBridge

HopeBridge Children & Elder Care is dedicated to supporting orphaned children and elders who cannot support themselves. We focus on essentials—food, shelter, school supplies, and basic necessities—delivered with dignity and respect.

Our Story & Mission

HopeBridge was created to make giving simple and trustworthy. We connect sponsors and volunteers with real needs through clear profiles, consistent updates, and respectful storytelling. Our goal is to strengthen stability—supporting education for children and essential care for elders.

Sponsorship is available as a $25/month subscription per child or elder, with a one-time donation option for urgent or seasonal needs.

Our Values

  • Dignity: We protect privacy and share stories respectfully.
  • Transparency: Clear information about how support is used.
  • Consistency: Reliable updates and steady, practical help.
  • Community: Sponsors and volunteers working together.

Transparency: How Funds Are Used

We believe trust is built through clarity. The information below explains how support is handled and how we share impact with sponsors and visitors.

Where the $25/month Goes

Sponsorship supports essentials such as food, basic household needs, hygiene items, school supplies, and other priority needs identified for each child or elder.

Updates & Impact

Sponsors receive periodic updates showing how support was used (progress notes, receipts when available, and story updates shared with permission).

Profile Selection & Privacy

Profiles are shared with consent and limited personal details. We prioritize safety and dignity, especially for children.

One-Time Donations

One-time gifts support urgent needs, events, and community projects. We list the purpose of each campaign whenever possible.
