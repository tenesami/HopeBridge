Sponsor a Child ($25/month)

Your monthly sponsorship helps provide essentials such as food, school supplies, hygiene items, and other needs identified for each child. Sponsors receive periodic updates shared respectfully and with permission.

What your $25/month can support

  • School supplies and basic learning materials
  • Meals and nutrition support
  • Clothing and hygiene essentials
  • Emergency or seasonal needs (as approved)

Sponsor an Elder ($25/month)

FAQ

Is sponsorship really $25/month?

Yes. Sponsorship is a $25/month subscription per child or elder. You can sponsor more than one profile.

Can I donate without subscribing?

Yes. You can make a one-time donation in any amount through the One-Time Donation section.

How do I choose who to support?

You can view profiles in the Stories section (or sponsor the “greatest need”).

How do I know my support made a difference?

We share impact updates and transparency notes, including what funds provided and progress updates.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Sponsorship can be cancelled at any time. Use the Manage/Cancel section or contact us.

Do you protect privacy?

Yes. We share only respectful updates with permission, especially for children and vulnerable elders.
